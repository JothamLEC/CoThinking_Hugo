---
title: Why does the blog look so rudimentary?
date: 2024-10-17T08:11:46.100Z
---
When building websites for clients, the market has fixed expectations regarding the aesthetics, content and even the tracking of the website.

But a personal website doesn't demand such deliberation. It gives you ultimate freedom to dictate the website's direction.

Hence, I opt for simplicity — a slice of the internet where your ideas are the only thing that matters, whilst everything else is a distraction.

I'm currently using the [Hugo Bear theme](https://github.com/janraasch/hugo-bearblog), and it's heavily inspired by Y Combinator's co-founder [Paul Graham's website](https://paulgraham.com/index.html). It's one of the best I've come across — especially his ability to condense complex topics into simple language, hosted on an out-dated yet efficient looking site, yet reaching tens of thousands of visitors every day.

I hope that by visiting my website, you've managed to take a breath of fresh air — away from sites with bloated scripts, ads and tactics used to distance you from the content you need.
