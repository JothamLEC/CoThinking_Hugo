### Why does the website look so basic?

When providing marketing services, I've used various themes, site-builders and plugins to put my clients in the best light possible. But here it's different — I want to build a website where words matter the most.

Check out Y Combinator's co-founder [Paul Graham's](https://paulgraham.com/) website — it's one of the best I've come across. He's managed to package substantially complex topics into simple language, sharing them on his similarly simple website.
